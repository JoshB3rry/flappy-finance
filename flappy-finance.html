🧠 Money words to know

You'll see these in the quiz — learn them and the bonus cash is yours.

📋 Budget

A plan for your money: what comes in, what goes out, and what's left to save.

🍔 Need vs. Want

Needs keep you alive & learning. Wants are fun extras — nice, not necessary.

📈 Interest

Extra money a bank pays you for saving — your money makes money.

🛟 Emergency Fund

Savings set aside for surprises, like a broken phone screen or a lost bike lock.

🛍️ Impulse Buy

Buying something unplanned just because it looks cool in the moment.

🎯 Savings Goal

A target amount + a deadline. Goals turn “someday” into a plan.

💡 Smart Money Tip

Try the 24-hour rule: see something you want? Wait a day. If you still want it tomorrow, it might be worth it. If you forgot it — you just saved money for free.

👨‍👩‍👧 For grown-ups & teachers

Covers budgeting, needs vs. wants, saving, interest, and smart spending for grades 6–8. Play together and ask: “What would YOU save for with $100?” No accounts, no purchases — just play and discuss.
